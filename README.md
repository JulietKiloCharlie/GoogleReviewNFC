# Google Review NFC Dashboard

A tap of the card opens a page made for that business. The customer rates the visit in a few seconds. Four and five stars go straight to Google Reviews. Three stars or fewer stay private: a short note is emailed to the business, and Syntrex Group Limited does not store it.

Staff run every client from one dashboard. Add the business, choose its Google listing, publish the page, and write that link to an NFC card. Each new client is another card on the same service.

## For the customer

- The card opens that business’s own page, with its name, logo, and welcome.
- Four and five stars open the official Google review page.
- Three stars or fewer open a private note for the business.

## For the team

- One private dashboard for every business.
- A permanent page address for each card.
- The Google listing fills in the review link.
- Private feedback goes to that business’s inbox.
